## The culture behind "Irashamaiseee" (いらっしゃいませ)

Has come to my attention this phrase when I start reading the book, everytime someones enter to the convenience store, a staff member say it to the customer, literally means "please do come".

At beginning I thought that was like a corporate dictated by the corporate who owns the store, but whas not the case, its a cultural practice with the aim to make the guest feel valued and welcome. 

Digging deeper, this practice reflects the japanese culture to be more precise **"the omotenashi"**, the japanese philosophy of wholehearted hospitality and service. It has its roots in the Heian period to be more precise in the tea ceremony, where both the host and the guest act with utmost sincerity.

I hope when I travel to Japan, I'll confirm that.
